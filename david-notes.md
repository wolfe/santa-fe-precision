# Santa Fe Precision — opening outline

Short opening-bid outline. Suits are ♠ ♥ ♦ ♣. In a shape, `=` locks the suits on each side and `-` lets those two lengths trade places. `2=2=5-4` is 2 spades, 2 hearts, and the minors in either order. `3-1=5-4` is the majors in either order and the minors in either order. `4-4-4-1` is a singleton in any suit. `4=4=1=4` is a singleton diamond.

## 1♣ — 17+ balanced, or 16+ unbalanced

A balanced 16 opens 1NT, not 1♣.

- **1♦** — 0–7, any shape.
  - **1M** — a 5-card major, or a 4-card major with a void or singleton. Forcing one round. Opener’s later new suits are game-forcing.
    - **1NT** — 0–5 dummy points, no support.
    - **2♣** — 5–7 dummy points, no support.
      - **2♦** — artificial. Opener had a minimum.
    - **2♦** — 3-card support, 5–7 dummy points.
    - **2M** — 4+ card support, 0–4 dummy points.
    - **3M** — 4+ card support, 5–7 dummy points.
    - **2NT** — 4+ card support, 8+ dummy points.
    - **Jump shift** — 5–7, a 6-card suit.
    - **Double jump** — splinter.
  - **1NT** — 17–18.
  - **2NT** — 21–23.
  - **2m** — usually a 6-card minor.
- **1♥** — 8–11, artificial, game-forcing. By a passed hand this is natural, 5+ hearts.
- **1♠, 2♣, 2♦, 2♥** — 12+, a 5-card suit. Option: start 1♠ at 8+.
- **1NT** — 12+, no 5-card suit. Option: swap 1NT and 2♥.
- **2♠** — 12+, any 4-4-4-1. 2NT asks for the singleton.

## Pass–pass–1♣

Positive responses are 8–10 and game-forcing. 1♦ is still 0–7.

- **1♥, 1♠, 1NT, 2♣, 2♦** — natural.
- **2♠** — any 4-4-4-1.
- **2♥** — not a bid.

## 1♣ opener’s second bid

- **1NT** — 17–18.
- **2NT** — 21–23. Over a two-level response, 2NT is 17–18 or 21–23. Do not jump.
- **A suit** — a new suit is 5+ cards. Rebidding a suit shows 6+.

## 1♣ doubled

- **Pass** — 0–4. **1♦** — 5–7.
- **Redouble** — 8+, no 5-card suit.
- **1NT** — 8+ balanced, and you want the lead.
- **A suit** — 8+ and a 5-card suit.

## 1♣, then a one-level overcall

- **Pass** — 0–4, or a trap pass.
- **Double** — 5–7, or 8+ with 4-4-4-1 and the singleton not in their suit.
- **Cue-bid** — 8+ balanced, no stopper in their suit.
- **Jump cue-bid** — 8+, 4-4-4-1, singleton in their suit.
- **1NT** — 8+ with a stopper in their suit.

## 1♣, then a two-level overcall

Same as a one-level overcall, except the double is 6–7, or 8+ balanced with no stopper in their suit. The 4-4-4-1 hand is still the jump cue-bid.

## 1♦ — 11–13 balanced, or 11–15 with diamonds, with both minors, or a 3-suiter that includes diamonds

- **1M** — 6+ points, possibly fewer, 4+ cards.
  - **1♠** over 1♥ — 4 spades and not 4 hearts. Then XYZ.
  - **2M** — minimum raise: 4+ card support, 11–14 dummy points, losing-trick count 7.
    - **2NT** — game try.
  - **2♥** over 1♠ only — 5 hearts and 6+ diamonds, not forcing.
  - **3M** — 15–17 dummy points, losing-trick count 6, usually a singleton.
  - **1NT** — no 4-card major. Then XYZ.
  - **2♣** — 5-4 in the minors. Must have 4+ diamonds.
  - **2♦** — 11–13, 6+ diamonds, losing-trick count 7.
  - **2NT** — 6+ diamonds and 3-card support for the major, 14–15, losing-trick count 6.
  - **3♣** — 5-5 in the minors, 14–15, losing-trick count 5. Not forcing.
  - **3♦** — like 2NT, but fewer than 3-card support.
- **1NT** — 7 to a bad 11, balanced, no 4-card major.
- **2♣** — 4+ clubs, forcing to game unless responder rebids 3♣.
- **2♦** — 4+ diamonds, forcing to game unless responder rebids 3♣ or 3♦.
- **2♥** — 5–9, 5 spades and 4 or 5 hearts.
- **2♠** — 10–12, 5 spades and 4 or 5 hearts.
- **2NT** — a good 11 to a bad 13, balanced, stoppers in ♣, ♥, and ♠, no 4-card major. Invites game.
- **3♣** — 5-4 in the minors, 5–9, preemptive, no 4-card major.
- **4♣** — more preemptive, 5-5 in the minors.
- **3♦** — 6+ diamonds, 5–9.
- **4♦** — the same idea with more diamonds.
- **3M** — preemptive. A hand that would have opened 3M.
- **3NT** — a good 13 to 15, natural, stoppers, no 4-card major.
- **4M** — 7+ cards, to play.

## Other openings

- **1♥ / 1♠** — 11–15, 5+ cards.
- **1NT** — 14–16, balanced.
- **2♣** — 11–15, 6+ clubs.
- **2♦** — a 3-suiter, short in diamonds.
- **2♥ / 2♠** — 4–9, 6+ cards.
- **2NT** — 19–20, balanced.

## General rules

- **Stayman** is on when no suit has been bid naturally yet.
- **Transfers** are on when no suit has been bid naturally, and responder is the one transferring.
- **XYZ** applies after 1♦ – 1x – 1x.
- **No Bergen.** Four-card support uses 2NT, not a Bergen raise.
- **Notrump shape.** Balanced, and also `2=2=5-4` or `4-2=5-2` so the 5-card suit is a minor. A singleton king may be `4-4-4-1` or `5-4-3-1`.
