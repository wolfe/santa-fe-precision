# Santa Fe Precision — Ritz notes

Fuller notes from `ritz-notes.pdf`. Suits are ♠ ♥ ♦ ♣. In a shape, `=` locks the suits on each side and `-` lets those two lengths trade places. `2=2=5-4` is 2 spades, 2 hearts, and the minors in either order. `3-1=5-4` is the majors in either order and the minors in either order. `4-4-4-1` is a singleton in any suit. `4=4=1=4` is a singleton diamond. Meaning changes from the draft are listed at the end.

## Opening bids

| Bid | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **1♣** | 16+ points, any shape. A balanced 16 opens 1NT. |
| **1♦** | 11–15, 2+ diamonds. Announce “could be short.” |
| **1♥** | 11–15, 5+ hearts |
| **1♠** | 11–15, 5+ spades. Option: 8+ points. |
| **1NT** | 14–16, balanced. Option: switch this with 2♥. |
| **2♣** | 11–15, 6+ clubs |
| **2♦** | 11–15, void or singleton diamond, no 5-card major |
| **2♥** | 4–9, 6+ hearts. Option: switch this with 1NT. |
| **2♠** | 4–9, 6+ spades |
| **2NT** | 19–20, balanced |

## 1♣ — 16+ points, any shape

Forcing one round. It says nothing about clubs.

### 1♦ — 0–7, any shape

- **1M** — a 4-card major in a 4-4-4-1, or a 5+ card major. No upper limit. Forcing one round. The major may be only four cards.
  - **1♠** over 1♥ — cannot support hearts. Forcing one round.
  - **1NT** — 0–5, cannot support the major. Forcing one round.
  - **2♣** — 6–7, cannot support the major. Forcing one round.
    - **2♦** — artificial weak relay. One of two ways to stop below game. The other is to rebid the major.
      - Responder bids, or passes with 5+ diamonds. A pass is allowed only with 5 diamonds.
    - **2M** — rebidding the major is not forcing. Bidding the other major is forcing to game.
    - **2NT** — forcing. Waiting to see what responder bids next.
    - **3m** — 4-card major and a 5+ card minor, forcing to game.
  - **2♦** — 5–7 dummy points, 3-card support. Forcing one round. Opener bids 2 or 4 of the major.
  - **2M** — 0–4 raise. Forcing one round.
  - **3M** — 5–7 jump raise, invitational. Forcing one round.
  - **2NT** — 8+ dummy points. Forcing one round.
  - **Jump in a new suit** — 5–7, a 6+ card suit. Forcing one round.
  - **Double jump in a new suit** — splinter raise. Forcing one round.
- **1NT** — 17–18, balanced. Also `2=2=5-4` or `4-2=5-2`, so the 5-card suit is a minor. Also `4-4-4-1` or `5-4-3-1` when the singleton is a king.
  - After this artificial auction, Stayman and transfers are on.
- **2m** — 16–21, usually 6+ cards, no 4-card major, not forcing. With `2=2=5-4`, bid notrump instead.
- **2♥** — 5+ hearts, forcing to game.
  - **2♠** — automatic inquiry.
    - **2NT / 3NT / 4NT** — 24–26 / 27–29 / 30+.
    - **3♣ / 3♦** — 4 clubs or 4 diamonds.
    - **3♥** — 6+ hearts, no second suit.
    - **3♠** — 6+ hearts and 4+ spades.
- **2♠** — 5+ spades, forcing to game.
  - **2NT** — automatic waiting bid, unless you can raise spades.
    - **3♣ / 3♦** — a 4+ card suit.
    - **3♥** — 6+ spades and 4+ hearts.
    - **3♠** — 6+ spades, no second suit.
- **2NT** — 21–23, balanced. Same shapes as the 1NT rebid.
- **3♣ / 3♦** — a 5+ card suit, no 4+ card major, game-forcing. Responses are natural.
- **3♥ / 3♠** — rare, a self-sufficient suit. Asks responder to bid controls up the line.
- **3NT** — rare, to play.

### Positive responses

- **1♥** — 8–11, any shape, game-forcing.
- **1♠** — 12+, 5+ spades, game-forcing. Option: start this at 8+.
- **1NT** — 12+, no 5+ card suit, game-forcing. Stayman is on. Transfers are off, so the strong hand declares.
- **2♣** — 12+, 5+ clubs, game-forcing.
- **2♦** — 12+, 5+ diamonds, game-forcing.
- **2♥** — 12+, 5+ hearts, game-forcing. Option: swap this with 1NT.
- **2♠** — 12+, any 4-4-4-1, game-forcing.
  - **2NT** asks for the singleton. Responder bids it. Opener then sets trump, and the partnership cue-bids.
  - **3♣ / 3♦ / 3♥ / 3♠** — a 6+ card suit, no 4+ card side suit. Willing to play there if partner has a low singleton. Cue-bid to look for slam.

### Opener’s rebid after a positive response other than 1♦

- A new suit shows 5+ cards. Opener’s second new suit is 4+ cards.
- A jump is a splinter raise.
- A suit rebid after two new suits shows 6+ cards. Avoid rebidding a 5-card suit.
- With 4-4-4-1, opener rebids an artificial 2♠.
- **1NT** — 17–18, balanced.
  - After the artificial 1♥ response, Stayman and transfers are both on.
  - After a natural positive, Stayman is on and transfers are off.
- **2NT** — 21–23, balanced. If the response was already at the two level, 2NT is 17–18 or 21–23. Do not jump over a two-level bid.

## Pass–pass–1♣

Still 16+, any shape. Positive bids are 8–10, because a stronger hand would have opened.

- **1♦** — 0–7, any shape.
- **1♥ / 2♣ / 2♦** — 8–10, a 5+ card suit, game-forcing.
- **2♥** — not a bid.
- **1NT** — 8–10, balanced, no 5-card suit.
- **1♠** — 8–10, 5+ spades, game-forcing.
- **2♠** — 8–10, any 4-4-4-1.

## 1♣ in competition

### They overcall

- **Pass** — 0–4.
- **Double** — 5–7, or 8+ with 4-4-4-1 and the singleton not in their suit.
  - A new suit is 5+ cards and forcing one round.
  - **1NT** — 17–18 with a stopper. Jump to 2NT with 21–23. Stayman and transfers are on.
  - **Pass** — convert with four trump tricks. Rare.
- **A new suit** — 8+, a 5+ card suit, game-forcing.
- **1NT** — 8+, balanced, no 5-card suit.
- **Cue-bid** — 8+, balanced, no stopper.
- **Jump cue-bid** — 8+, 4-4-4-1, singleton in their suit.

### They double

- **1♦** — 5–7, any shape. Opener’s next bid is forcing one round.
- **Redouble** — 8+, no 5+ card suit, game-forcing.
- **1NT** — 8+, balanced, game-forcing. Stayman is on. Transfers are off.

## 1♦ — the catch-all

1♦ is defined by what it is not:

- no 5-card major unless the diamonds are longer
- no 6+ clubs
- not a balanced 14–16
- not a 3-suiter with a void or singleton diamond

That leaves 11–13 balanced or semi-balanced, or 11–15 with diamonds, with both minors, or a 3-suiter that includes diamonds.

It usually shows 2+ diamonds. It can be 4=4=1=4 with the ♦A or ♦K and 11–13. Treat that hand as balanced. It is rare.

### After 1♥ — 6+ points, possibly fewer, 4+ hearts

- **1♠** — 4 spades and not 4 hearts. XYZ applies.
- **1NT** — no 4-card major. XYZ applies.
- **2♣** — at least 5-4 in the minors. You must have 4 diamonds.
- **2♦** — 11–13, usually 6+ diamonds.
- **2♥** — 11–14 dummy points, 4-card support.
  - **2NT** — game try. “Do you like your hand?”
- **3♥** — 15–17 dummy points. Virtually guarantees a singleton.
- **2NT** — 14–15, 3-card heart support, usually 6+ diamonds.
- **3♣** — a maximum short of a 1♣ opening, 5+ clubs and 5+ diamonds, not forcing.
- **3♦** — 14–15, fewer than 3-card heart support (with 3-card support, bid 2NT), usually 6+ diamonds.

### After 1♠ — 6+ points, possibly fewer, 4+ spades

- **1NT** — no 4-card major. XYZ applies.
- **2♠** — 11–14 dummy points, 4-card support.
  - **2NT** — game try.
- **3♠** — 15–17 dummy points. Virtually guarantees a singleton.
- **2♣** — at least 5-4 in the minors, with 4 diamonds.
- **2♦** — 11–13, usually 6+ diamonds.
- **2♥** — 5 hearts and 6+ diamonds. Not forcing.
- **2NT** — 14–15, 3-card spade support, usually 6+ diamonds.
- **3♣** — maximum short of a 1♣ opening, 5-5 in the minors, not forcing.
- **3♦** — 14–15, fewer than 3-card spade support, usually 6+ diamonds.

### Other responses

- **1NT** — 7 to a bad 11, no 4-card major.
- **2♣** — 4+ clubs, game-forcing unless opener rebids 3♣. Responder rarely has a 4+ card major, so opener’s 2♥ and 2♠ are artificial. Responder has to find a major fit.
  - **2♦** — 5+ diamonds, unbalanced, fewer than 4 clubs.
  - **2♥** — 11–13, balanced. Nothing about hearts. A direct 3-level bid is the fast route. 2♠ is the slow route.
    - **2♠** — one to three low cards in a side suit. A puppet to 2NT. This is also how responder creates a game force: 2♠, then 3♣. An immediate 3♣ would only invite.
      - **2NT** completes the puppet.
        - **3♣** — 6+ clubs, forcing, balanced or short in diamonds.
          - **3♦** asks. **3♥** is balanced. **3♠** is short in diamonds.
        - **3♦** — forcing, clubs and diamonds, clubs longer. Usually 5 clubs and 4 diamonds.
        - **3♥** — forcing, 6+ clubs, short hearts.
        - **3♠** — forcing, 6+ clubs, short spades.
    - **2NT** — balanced, slam interest, asking for a 4-card minor.
    - **3♣** — 10–12, 6+ clubs, invitational.
    - **3♦** — 5-5 in the minors, game-forcing.
    - **3♥ / 3♠** — 6+ clubs and a 4+ card major, game-forcing.
    - **3NT** — to play.
  - **2♠** — 4+ clubs, void or singleton in spades or hearts.
    - **2NT** asks where. **3♣** is hearts. **3♦** is spades.
  - **2NT** — 11–12, 4=4=4=1, and the singleton club is not an honor. With a club honor, treat it as balanced and bid 2♥.
  - **3♣** — 11–13, `2=2=5-4`.
  - **3♦** — 6+ diamonds, a maximum for a non-1♣ opener.
  - **3♥ / 3♠** — 6+ diamonds and a 5+ card major.
  - **3NT** — same shape as 2NT, but 14–15. With 13 points, use judgment.
- **2♦** — 4+ diamonds, game-forcing unless opener rebids 3♣ or 3♦.
  - **2♥** — 11–13, balanced. Nothing about hearts. Direct 3-level bids are the fast route. 2♠ is the slow route.
    - **2♠** — one to three low cards in a side suit. Puppet to 2NT, or the start of a game force.
      - **2NT** completes the puppet.
        - **3♣** — game-forcing, 5-4 in the minors.
        - **3♦** — game-forcing, 6+ diamonds, balanced or short in clubs.
          - **3♥** asks. **3♠** is balanced. **3NT** is short in clubs.
        - **3♥ / 3♠** — game-forcing, 6+ diamonds, short in hearts or in spades.
    - **3♣** — invitational, 5-4 in the minors.
    - **3♦** — invitational, 6+ diamonds.
    - **3♥ / 3♠** — invitational, 6+ diamonds and a 4+ card major.
  - **2♠** — 4+ diamonds, void or singleton in one of the other suits.
    - **2NT** asks where. **3♣** clubs, **3♦** hearts, **3♥** spades.
  - **2NT** — 6+ diamonds, semi-balanced, no void or singleton.
  - **3♣** — `4-1=3=5`: 5 clubs, 3 diamonds, and a void or singleton in a major.
    - **3♦** — not forcing.
    - **3♥** asks where the shortage is. **3♠** is hearts. **3NT** is spades.
    - **4♣ / 4♦** — sets trump.
    - **4♥ / 4♠** — Roman Keycard for clubs, or for diamonds.
- **2♥** — 5–9, 5 spades and 4 or 5 hearts. UMJOODO: Unusual Majors Jumps Over a Diamond Opening.
- **2♠** — 10–12, invitational, the same major shape.
- **2NT** — a good 11 to a bad 13, balanced, stoppers, no 4+ card major.
- **3♣ / 4♣** — preemptive in the minors, 5-4. 4♣ is 5-5 and more preemptive. No 4+ card major.
- **3♦ / 4♦** — preemptive, 6+ diamonds. 4♦ shows more diamonds.
- **3♥ / 3♠** — preemptive in the major.
- **3NT** — a good 13 to 15, balanced, stoppers, no 4+ card major.
- **4♥ / 4♠** — a natural 7+ card suit, to play.

### They double 1♦

- **Redouble** — 10+, no good 4+ card suit to bid.
- **2♣** — like a weak two-bid. Not forcing.
- **2♦** — same as without the double: 4+ cards, game-forcing unless opener rebids 3♣ or 3♦.
- **2♥ / 2♠** — same UMJOODO bids as without the double.
- **2NT** — same as without the double: 11–13, balanced, no 4-card major, stoppers.
- **3♣** — same preemptive 5-4 in the minors.
- **3♦** — same preemptive 6+ diamonds.

### They overcall 1♦

- **A new suit at the one level** — 6+, 5+ cards.
- **A new suit at the two level** — 10+, 5+ cards.
- **Double** — negative, 4 cards in the other major.
- **1NT** — 7–11, balanced. Might have a stopper.
- **2♦** — 10+, 5+ diamonds.
- **2 of the other major** — 6+ in the major, game-forcing.
- **2NT** — natural and forcing. Denies a 4-card major. Promises a stopper.
- **3♣** — preemptive, 5-4 in the minors.
- **3♦** — preemptive, 6+ diamonds.
- **4♣** — more preemptive, 5-5 in the minors.

## Most common auctions after 1♦

A direct 3-level bid is the fast route. Bidding 2♠ first, then a 3-level bid, is the slow route.

| Club one-suiter | Forcing | 2♣, then slow 3♣, 3♥, or 3♠ |
| Diamond one-suiter | Invitational | 2♦, then fast 3♦ |
| Diamond one-suiter | Forcing | 2♦, then slow 3♦, 3♥, or 3♠ |

| Both minors | Forcing | 2♦, then slow 3♦ with 4 clubs and 5 diamonds |

## 1♥ and 1♠ — 11–15, 5+ cards

1♠ may be opened on 8+ as an option.

- **1♠** over 1♥ — natural, 4+ spades.
- **1NT** — 6–12, not 4 spades, semi-forcing. Partner may pass with 11–13 and a balanced hand.
  - Rebid the major with 6+ cards.
  - A new suit shows 4+ cards.
  - Pass with 11–13 and any 5-3-3-2.
- **A new suit at the two level** — forcing to game.
  - Rebidding the major promises 6+ cards.
  - A new suit at the two level promises 4+ cards.
  - **2NT** — waiting, when nothing else fits.
  - A new suit at the three level promises 5+ cards.

### Raising partner

With 3-card support:

- 6–7: 1NT, then 2 of the major
- 8–10: 2 of the major
- 11–12: 1NT, then 3 of the major
- 13: 4 of the major if slam is unlikely, or a freakish shape, or a two-over-one in a minor

With 4-card support:

- 4–6: 3 of the major, preemptive
- 7–9: jump to the bid one under the major, a mixed raise
- 10+: 2NT, modified Jacoby
- 13–15: a double-jump splinter

With 5-card support:

- up to 12 points: 4 of the major
- 13+: splinter

In competition the raises are mostly the same. A cue-bid is a limit raise or better with 3-card support. If that cue-bid collides with the mixed raise, bid 2NT with 3-card support. The mixed raise takes precedence.

A passed hand may use Drury. It is not required.

## 2♣ — 11–15, 6+ clubs

May include a 5+ card major. With weak clubs, no void or singleton, and 14–15, open 1NT instead.

- **2♦** — invitational or better. Asks for more.
  - **2♥ / 2♠** — a 4+ card suit, minimum or maximum.
  - **2NT** — no 4+ card major, balanced maximum. Implies good clubs, since the hand was not opened 1NT.
  - **3♣** — no 4+ card major, minimum, balanced or unbalanced.
  - **3♦ / 3♥ / 3♠** — maximum, short in the bid suit, forcing to game.
- **2♥** — 10+, 5+ hearts, forcing one round.
  - **2NT** — maximum, no 3-card support. Could be a singleton heart with the other suits stopped.
    - **3♣** — 3+ clubs, game-forcing.
    - **3♦** — 4+ diamonds, game-forcing.
    - **3♥** — 6+ hearts, game-forcing.
    - **3♠** — 4+ spades, game-forcing.
  - **3♣** — minimum, no 3-card support.
    - **3♦** — 4+ diamonds, game-forcing.
    - **3♥** — 6+ hearts, game-forcing.
    - **3♠** — 4+ spades, game-forcing.
  - **3♥** — minimum, 3-card support, not forcing.
  - **4♥** — more than a minimum, 3-card support.
- **2♠** — 10+, 5+ spades, forcing one round.
- **2NT** — limit raise or better, 4+ clubs.
  - **3♣** — minimum, balanced.
    - Pass with a balanced hand.
    - **3♦ / 3♥ / 3♠** — short in that suit. Opener bids 4♣ or 5♣.
  - **3NT** — maximum, balanced. Opener chooses 3NT or 5♣.
- **3♣** — 6–9, 3 clubs.
- **3♦ / 3♥ / 3♠** — 4+ clubs, void or singleton in the bid suit, game-forcing.
- **3NT** — to play. 14+, balanced, stoppers, no 4-card major.
- **4♣** — preemptive.
- **4♦** — Kickback.
- **5♣** — preemptive.

### Interference over 2♣

- After a double, the bids keep their meanings. Redouble shows 10+ and a defensive hand, ready to penalize.
- After an overcall: negative double. A new suit at the two level is 5+ cards and forcing one round. A raise is a club raise. A new suit at the three level is 5+ cards and forcing to game. A cue-bid is a good club raise, forcing to game. 2NT is to play and shows a stopper.

## 2♦ — 11–15, short diamonds

Three suits, with a void or singleton diamond that is not the ace or king. The shapes are `4=4=1=4`, `4=4=0=5`, and `4-3=1=5`.

- **Pass** — to play, despite the diamond shortage.
- **2♥ / 2♠ / 3♣** — to play.
- **3♦** — invitational, 6+ diamonds.
- **3♥ / 3♠** — a 5+ card suit, forcing, sets trump.
- **4♥ / 4♠** — preemptive, or a perfect fit to play.
- **2NT** — asks for more.
  - **3♣** — 11–12, or a poor 13. Every minimum.
    - **3♦** asks for the shape.
      - **3♥** is 4=3=1=5.
      - **3♠** is 3=4=1=5.
      - **3NT** is 4=4=1=4 or 4=4=0=5.
  - **3♦** — maximum, 4=4=1=4.
  - **3♥** — maximum, 4=3=1=5.
  - **3♠** — maximum, 3=4=1=5.
  - **3NT** — maximum, 4=4=0=5.

### Mulberry after the 2NT response

The simple version is easier and has no slam tries. The original version allows slam tries.

Simple:

- **4♣** — puppet to 4♦. Then 4♥, 4♠, or 5♣ is a signoff.
- **4♦** — Roman Keycard for clubs.
- **4♥** — Roman Keycard for diamonds.
- **4♠** — Roman Keycard for hearts.
- **4NT** — Roman Keycard for spades.

Original:

- **4♣** — puppet to 4♦. Then 4♥, 4♠, 4NT, or 5♣ is a natural slam try. Partner accepts by bidding the next step as Roman Keycard.
- **4♦** — puppet to 4♥. Pass, or correct to 4♠ or 5♣ to play.
- **4♥** — Roman Keycard for clubs.
- **4♠** — Roman Keycard for hearts.
- **4NT** — Roman Keycard for spades.

Mulberry is also used after 1♣–1♥–2♠, after 1♣–2♠ (the 4-4-4-1), and after 1♣–(1♠)–3♠ showing 1=4=4=4, plus other cases. See book page 106.

## XYZ

Used after 1♦ – 1x – 1x, for invitations and game forces. It applies over interference.

- **2♦** — game-forcing. Then natural.
- **2♥** — 6–9, to play. Opener must pass.
- **2♠** — not a bid.
- **3♣** — 6–9, 5+ clubs and 4 hearts, to play. Opener must pass.
- **3♦** — a 5+ card suit and 5+ hearts. Slam try.
- **3♥** — a 6+ card suit. Slam try.

### 1♦ – 1♠ – 1NT

- **2♣** — invitational. Forces opener to bid 2♦. Then natural.
- **2♦** — game-forcing. Then natural.
- **2♥** — 5+ spades and 4+ hearts, less than invitational. Opener passes or corrects to 2♠.
- **2♠** — 6–9, to play. Opener must pass.
- **3♣** — 6–9, 5+ clubs and 4 spades, to play. Opener must pass.
- **3♦ / 3♥** — a 5+ card suit and 5+ spades. Slam try.
- **3♠** — a 6+ card suit. Slam try.

## Support doubles

Through 2♠ on opener’s right. Partner has bid 1♥ or 1♠. What the left-hand opponent did does not matter.

The double shows 3-card support. Pass when you are weak, or when defending is better. Think of it as a takeout double.

After 1♦ – pass – 1♥ – 1♠ – double – pass, responder bids:

- **1NT, 2♣, 2♦, 2♥** — not forcing, up to 10 points.
- **2NT, 3♥** — invitational.
- **2♠**, or a jump in a new suit — game-forcing. The 1♦ opener may have only two diamonds, so a diamond bid needs five.

Over a one-level bid, raise with a fit. A new suit shows 5+ cards. Bid 1NT even with no stopper. A cue-bid forces to game.

Over a two-level bid, you may have to rebid the major with only four cards.

Opener’s later bids, with strength and a fit: 2♣ is natural and forcing. 3♦ shows 6+ diamonds and is game-forcing.

## Other major-suit agreements

- **Semi-forcing 1NT** — 6–12. Partner can pass with 11–13 and a balanced hand. The follow-ups are mostly natural.
- **Splinter raises of 1♥ or 1♠** — 13–15, 4+ card support, a void or singleton in the bid suit, losing-trick count 6 or 7. Too strong for that: bid Jacoby 2NT.
- **Modified Jacoby 2NT** — 10+, 4-card support, and not a splinter. On in competition. Opener rebids:
  - a void or singleton at the three level, regardless of strength, including 3♠ over 1♥–2NT
  - 3 of the major with 11–13, so a limit raise can pass
  - 4 of the major with 14–15

## 1NT auctions

The same structure applies in competition, and when a bid is a 1NT rebid rather than a 1NT opening.

**Stayman is on** when no suit has been bid naturally. When a suit has been bid naturally, 2♣ is Puppet Stayman. Example: 1♣ – 1♠ – 1NT – 2♣. The 1♠ was natural.

Stayman shows at least one 4-card major. It is 9+ points, unless the hand is 5-4 in the majors and will pass or rebid a major (crawling Stayman), or it is garbage Stayman.

### 1NT – 2♣

- **2♦** — no 4-card major.
  - **2M** — 5 of that major and 4 of the other. Expecting partner to pass.
  - **3M** — 5 and 4 in the majors, forcing to game. Or play Smolen and bid the 4-card suit.
  - **3m** — a 5+ card minor, forcing to game.
- **2♥** — 4 or 5 hearts, and maybe 4 spades.
  - **2♠** — 4 spades, invitational. Opener bids accordingly.
  - **2NT** — undefined.
  - **3♣ / 3♦** — forcing to game, likely a 5+ card suit.
  - **3♠ / 4♣ / 4♦** — splinter raises.
  - **4♠** — Kickback.
  - **4NT** — quantitative.
- **2♠** — 4 or 5 spades.
  - **2NT** — invitational. Implies 4 hearts.
  - **3♣ / 3♦** — same as over 2♥.
  - **4♣ / 4♦ / 4♥** — splinters.
  - **4NT** — quantitative.

### Jacoby transfers: 2♦ to hearts, 2♥ to spades

After 1NT – 2♦ – 2♥:

- **Pass** — to play.
- **2NT** — 5 hearts, invitational. Opener bids 3♥, 3NT, or 4♥.
- **2♠** — 5 spades and 4 hearts, invitational. Opener bids 3♥, 3♠, 4♥, or 4♠.
- **3♥** — 6+ hearts, invites 4♥.
- **3♠ / 4♣ / 4♦** — splinter with 6+ hearts.
- **3NT** — 5 hearts. Opener passes or corrects to 4♥.
- **3♣ / 3♦** — a 4+ card suit, forcing to game. Usually five cards, but four is allowed when 3NT is a poor description.
  - **3♥** — 3+ hearts.
  - **3NT** — to play.
  - A new suit is ambiguous: a stopper in the bid suit and none in the other, or a control. Bid 3NT with the stopper. Partner bids again if it was a control.
- **4NT** — quantitative. For Roman Keycard, transfer and then bid 4♠.

**1NT – 2♦ – 3♥** is a super-accept, 4+ card support.

**1NT – 2♥ – 2♠ – 3♥** is 5 spades and 5 hearts, forcing to game.

If the opponents double the transfer, pass with 2-card support and accept with 3+ card support.

### 2♠ — a range ask that may show clubs

Asks whether the 1NT is minimum or maximum. It may be inviting 3NT, or it may be 6+ clubs.

- **2NT** — minimum. **3♣** — maximum.
- Responder then corrects to 3NT or to clubs, or passes.
- Or **3♦** with a game force, 6+ clubs, and either a balanced hand or a void or singleton. **3♥** asks which. **3♠** is balanced. **3NT** is a void or singleton diamond.
- Or **3♥ / 3♠** with a game force, 6+ clubs, and a void or singleton in the bid suit.

### Puppet Stayman

Used with game values and one or two 3-card majors. A 4-card major is allowed. With a 5-card major, transfer instead.

- **3M** — opener has that 5-card major. Responder bids 3NT, 4 of the major, or a new suit as a control.
- **3♣** — denies a 5-card major. With a 4-card major as well, bid the major you do not hold, as a transfer.

### Other bids over 1NT

- **3♣** — transfer to 3♦. 6+ diamonds, no 4+ card major, any strength except an invitation. Invite with 2♠ instead. After 3♣–3♦: pass, bid a new suit to show a void or singleton, or bid 3NT as a slam try. A direct 1NT–3NT is not a slam try.
- **3♦** — 5+ diamonds and 5+ clubs, game-forcing. With both majors stopped, bid 3NT. With a minor fit, bid the minor. With neither, bid the major that is stopped. Optional: 3♥ asks for shortness, 3♠ shows short hearts, and 3NT shows short spades.
- **3♥ or 3♠** — a void or singleton in the bid suit, a 3-card major, and 5-4 or better in the minors, game-forcing. When Puppet Stayman applies, this bid carries more information. Bid the best fit.

### Transfer Lebensohl

If they double 1NT and the double is conventional, ignore it. Stayman, transfers, and the rest of the notrump structure stay on.

If the double is for penalty, the system is off. DONT runouts:

- **2♣** — clubs and a higher suit
- **2♦** — diamonds and a higher suit
- **2♥** — hearts and a higher suit
- **2♠** — spades
- **Redouble** — a 5+ card suit. Opener bids 2♣ as a relay.

If they overcall 2♣, all systems are on.

If they overcall 2♦, 2♥, or 2♠, transfers are off. A two-level bid is natural, 5+ cards. Double is takeout. 2NT starts Transfer Lebensohl.

- **1NT – overcall – 2NT** — Transfer Lebensohl. Partner must bid 3♣. Responder passes or corrects. This is how you bid a suit you could not show at the two level.
- **Three-level bids are transfers.** 3♣ shows 5+ diamonds, invitational or better. 3♦ shows 5+ hearts, invitational or better. 3♥ shows 5+ spades, invitational or better. 3♠ shows 6+ clubs, game-forcing.
- **3NT** — to play. By agreement it shows or denies a stopper. Many experts do neither, and let the opponents guess.
- **A transfer into their suit** — Stayman, promising at least one 4-card major, game-forcing.

## 2NT and 3NT openings

**2NT** is 19–20, balanced or semi-balanced. If you use the chapter 21 tweak, 1NT becomes 15–17 in some seats and this opening becomes 20–21.

Responses: Stayman, Jacoby transfers, 3♠ as minor-suit Stayman, 3NT to play, 4♦ and 4♥ as Texas transfers, and 4♣ as Gerber.

**3NT** is a gambling 3NT: a solid 7+ card minor. An outside ace or king is preferred, not required.
